The northern line begins with Proto-Ahéric and passes through Proto-Tū́glemća, Old Tū́glemća and Old Rákva to Classical Rákva, from <bdi>−9,000</bdi> to <bdi>−2,400</bdi> on the chronology of the northern kingdoms. Each stage is a set of sound laws applied in a fixed order; none of it is written by hand. Choose a word, then call up the laws one at a time.

{{tracer}}

The forms are produced by the engine that built the language volumes. Aspirates and labiovelars are written in the traditional manner (bʰ, gʷ), and stress is marked with an acute. The words are a small selection from a lexicon of 387 senses.
