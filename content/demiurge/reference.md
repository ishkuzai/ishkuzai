This is the guide to the whole project. Its sections follow the order in which Demiurge builds a world: first the principles and the scales of space and time, then the star system, the planet and its geology, the surface, the land, life and its history, intelligence, peoples and races, culture, the present-day world, realms, languages, game statistics, the output, the two defaults, and the framework that joins the modules.

Each section opens with its **status**. *Built* means it is working and tested in the current build; *partial* means some of what the project aims at is done. Where something is not built yet, the section says so under a **Still to build** heading, so that the scope is as plain as the progress.

The text is the project's own working document, revised as the project is; the technical detail lives in the module specifications it names. Use the search box to find a topic.
